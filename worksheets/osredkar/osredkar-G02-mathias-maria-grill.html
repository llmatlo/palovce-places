CHILDREN’S DETAILS

Child 1: Franz (Frank) Osredkar (b. 1865) - OUR DIRECT ANCESTOR

  • Born at Schuttenbach 3 (listed on immigration documents as his origin)
  • Does not reappear in local records after childhood
  • By ~1890: Moved to Bosnia. Occupation corrected 2026-09-03 per Lidija Sambunjak (professional researcher — source-docs/lidija-sambunjak-osredkar-preskar-children-letter-2026.md, restating what her original 2022 family group record already had in the children’s birth-book notes, source-docs/ludmilla-family-sheet-from-slovenia.md): the earlier “railroad/steel mill construction” guess is not what the actual Sarajevo church vital-book records show. Per his children’s birth records, Frank is listed at various points as a day laborer at the royal railway, a thrower (rail-track layer), a plain worker, and a locksmith — never steel-factory/steel-mill. He’s “nowhere listed as worked in the steel factory” per her direct archival check.
  • All four children born in Sarajevo per FindAGrave records (see G01 worksheet)
  • Ludmilla born 20 Sep 1894 (possibly before marriage — not listed as father on birth record)
  • 1903: Immigrated to United States
  • Grandmother Sophie Osredkar Laurich is his daughter

Child 2: Lucia Osredkar (b. 1867)

  • Married Franz Pirc
  • First child Maria born at Zale/Schuttenbach 3, 29 Apr 1888 (Barthol Rems godparent)
  • Children: Maria (1888), Johanna (1890 at Zale 6), Stephan (1894), Victor (1897), Anna (1899), Cecilja (1903)
  • Pirc family operates B&B in Zale area now

Child 3: Maria Osredkar (b. 1870)

  • Died age 3 in April 1873 from Scarlet Fever
  • Cousin Anton Rems also died same week (Scarlet Fever)

Child 4: Johann Osredkar (b. 1873)

  • Born during family crisis (sister just died, mother would die 6 months later)
  • Age 27 at Zale 3 for 1900 marriage to Luzia Zorman, age 20
  • Marriage: 5 Nov 1900, Zale 3 — live-verified 2026-09-05, Kamnik 03879 pg79. Luzia Zorman’s own details corrected via this record: b. 13 Dec 1877, Mlaka (house 21), parish Vodice — supersedes the earlier “~1879, from Terzin 20” (a misattributed cell in the secondary “Kamnik families v3” compilation; “Terzin, fara Mengeš” actually describes the groom’s own parish-of-banns).
  • Died 27 March 1944, Vienna — found 2026-09-05 via a margin annotation on his own birth entry (Kamnik 03875 pg167), citing the civil registry court in Marburg/Maribor. Supersedes the earlier bare “1944 per family records.”
