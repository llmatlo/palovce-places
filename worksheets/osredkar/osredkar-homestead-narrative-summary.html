Searching for Georg (Osredkar Homestead)

Original Author: Gary DelNero Document Version: 3.0, 13 December 2022 Original File: source-docs/osredkar-homestead-v2.md Summary Created: February 2026

Purpose of This Document

Gary’s narrative traces the Osredkar family from Zgornje Palovce (SP3, Budnar House Museum) back to their ancestral homesteads at Bela Pec 3 and Sovinja Pec 5 (Liukovo) in the parish of Smartno v Tuhinju. It is the foundational research document for the Osredkar direct line and the Pistotnik connection.

Key Findings

1. Georg Osredkar’s Origins Identified

  • Georg (b. 23 Mar 1777) was born at Sovinja Pec 5 (Liukovo), parish Smartno v Tuhinju
  • His cryptic marriage record notation “Luiko” / “Sello” finally decoded as Liukovo near Sella/Bela Pec
  • Father: Martinus Osredkar vulgo Liuk and Barbara Kuhar
  • Georg married Helena Rems in 1796 at Vranja Pec and settled at Zgornje Palovce SP3

2. Maria Osredkar (Vaupetic) Identified at Bela Pec 3

  • Maria Osredkar (b. 20 May 1777) born to Blais and Maria at Bela Pec 3 (Osredkar farm)
  • Married Joseph Vaupetic in 1796 — same year as Georg’s marriage
  • Both Georg and Maria entered Zgornje Palovce in 1796 from the Bela Pec area
  • They are NOT siblings — from different branches of the Osredkar family

3. Two Distinct Osredkar Homesteads

Property Location 1827 Owner Family Line
Bela Pec 3 (Osredek) Buildings 73-75, Osredek farm Stephan Pistotnik Maria’s line (Pistotnik vulgo Osredkar)
Sovinja Pec 5 (Liukovo) Building 51, cadastral Znojile Joseph Osredkar Georg’s line (Osredkar vulgo Liuk)
  • About 30 min walk between the two properties, separated by a river
  • About 2 hours walk from either property to Zgornje Palovce

4. Pistotnik/Osredkar Name Change Explained

  • Joseph Pistotnik (of Mathias Pistotnik from Gabria) married Elizabeth, daughter of Lucas Osredkar in 1730
  • Joseph adopted the Osredkar name upon marriage into the Osredkar farm at Bela Pec 3
  • Their son Blais (b. 1751) used both names: “Pistotnik vulgo Osredkar” and “Osredkar”
  • Maria kept the Osredkar name; her brother Stephan reverted to Pistotnik
  • Bartholomew Pistotnik (b. 1767, Blais’s brother — both sons of Josephus Pistotnik) married Helena Shumer in 1802 — their granddaughter Maria Grill married Mathias Osredkar (Gary’s 2G grandmother connection)
  • Key insight: Bartholomew descends from Pistotnik of Gabria + Prelesnik of Bresia, NOT from Lucas Osredkar — despite living at the Osredkar farm

5. Vulgo (Known-As) Names

  • Georg’s line: Osredkar vulgo Liuk — named after the Liukovo homestead at SP5
  • Maria’s line: Pistotnik vulgo Osredkar — Pistotnik family who married into Osredkar farm
  • The vulgo names track which homestead a family occupies, not blood descent

Gary’s Working Assumption — largely resolved 2026-08-02

The original assumption (“Gregory is father of SP5’s Gregor,” stated explicitly as without evidence) turned out to be close but not quite right: rather than two brothers named Gregory/Gregor each fathering a separate line, SP5’s Gregorius and BP3’s “Gregor” are the same man. Cross-verification of three overlapping children’s birth records (Martin, Ursula, Agnes) against real Matricula citations confirmed they’re the same baptismal entries, not two coincidentally similar families — which also validated the otherwise-uncited claim that Lucas/Luka (BP3’s founder) is this man’s son. See osredkar-G08-gregorius-marina.md for the full resolution and hypothesis c3bda747 (confidence: probable) in Acorn.

Still unresolved: Bartolomej Osredkar (~1647) as Gregorius’s father remains an uncited claim from the compiled document pistotniki-osredkarji, and Gregorius’s own birth/baptismal record naming his parents has never been located.

Open Research Questions (from this narrative)

  1. How did Georg and Maria, both born 1777 near Bela Pec, both marry into Zgornje Palovce in 1796? Was this coincidence or arranged?
  2. Why did the Pistotnik/Osredkar families use vulgo names differently? Maria kept Osredkar; Stephan used Pistotnik.
  3. Where was Lucas Osredkar born? Not found in records beginning 1664. Possibly born before records, in another parish, or under a different name.
  4. Where is Gabria? Mathias Pistotnik (Joseph’s father) was from Gabria — possibly west of Ljubljana.
  5. Where is the nearest castle? Gary notes this is the first community without a nearby castle.

Apollonia Prelesnik Connection

  • Apollonia Prelesnik (b. 9 Feb 1728, Wresia/Bresia near Kamnik) married Joseph Pistotnik ~1750
  • Prelesnik family found in Grabon in 1825 cadastral survey
  • Johann Prelesnik in Grabon may be Bartholomew Pistotnik’s uncle
  • Grabon is near where Mathias Osredkar’s second wife lived, near Schuttenbach 3

WWII Note

The narrative references a tragic WWII incident at the Livek (Liukovo) homestead where partisans killed four family members and a shepherdess. The Germans were subsequently blamed as well, illustrating the cycle of violence in wartime Slovenia.
