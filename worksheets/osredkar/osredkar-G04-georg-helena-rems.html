Wife Notes

  • Helena was the oldest child of Mathias Rems’s second marriage to Maria Kregar
  • Per Two-Mathias hypothesis (PROBABLE, resolved 2026-05-16): Helena’s father is Mathias Rems son of Martinus & Barbara (b. Feb 1739 — Kamnik 00710 pg16; d. 28 Aug 1798 at Rabensburg 3). See Two-Mathias Hypothesis
  • Her father’s TWO marriages:
    • 1st: Ursula Berlz/Werlizan (m. 6 Sept 1761, from SP and Rabensburg) — 8 children including Matthaus (1762), Gregorius (1766), Simon (1771). Ursula died 3 Oct 1775 at SP4 (Kamnik 00723 pg144), age 30, same day as her infant son Bartholomeus
    • 2nd: Maria Urbani Kregar of Kregarjevo (m. 22 Jan 1776 at SP4) — Helena + 6 siblings
  • NOTE: The earlier SP families chart annotation favored Mathias son of Antony & Catharina (b. 9 Feb 1733, d. 1810 SP4) as Helena’s father. That identification has been REJECTED — Antony’s Mathias was still married to Lucia Wesjak in 1776 (Lucia did not die until 11 Jan 1799 at Rabensburg 6), so he could not have been the widower who remarried Maria Kregar. Antony’s Mathias is a great-uncle, not a great-grandfather, in your direct line.
  • Helena born 9 May 1777 at SP4, ~16 months after the 2nd marriage; her father had been demonstrably at SP4 since at least Oct 1775 (Ursula’s death there)
  • Family at SP4 (not SP3 as previously noted)

Marriage Notes

  • DATE DISCREPANCY RESOLVED 2026-08-04: Confirmed 25 April 1796, not 3 February. Direct read of VP 03564 pg6: groom “Georg Osredkar… aus Vranja Peč,” bride “Helena R[e/a]nf” (surname cursive slightly rough but a strong phonetic match to Rems), dated 25 April under the 1796 year heading. The 3 Feb 1796 candidate (from Kamnik 00733) was independently disproven the same session — that date’s groom is named “Josef,” confirming the old suspicion that 3 Feb belonged to a different couple (possibly Simon Rems), not Georg. Confidence “High” rather than “Confirmed” pending a sharper re-read of the bride’s exact surname spelling.
  • Georg’s origin noted cryptically as “Luiko” - confirmed as Sovinja Pec 5

Children Notes

  • 13 children total per SP families chart, born 1798-1822
  • At least 5 died young: Mathias (age 13), Maria (age 6), Ursula (age 14), Maria Anna (infant), Marianna (age 1)
  • Maria Osredkar (b. 1807) is the direct ancestor in this lineage. She never married but had 4 children at SP3:
    • Maria (b. 1 July 1825)
    • Franziskus (b. 12 Nov 1830)
    • Johann (b. 29 Aug 1835)
    • Mathias (b. 25 Feb 1838) — OUR ANCESTOR
  • Michael inherited SP3 per 1827 survey. First married Gertrude? Mrak (marriage record not found in VP). Second marriage 20 Jul 1851 to Anna (Lucas and Margaretha Rope) Kladnik, age 27. Their daughter Katharina (b. ~1852) married Brun, 1892.
  • Michael is listed as owner of SP3 in 1827 Habsburg cadastral survey. Georg Vaup owned it before. This is building #10 in the cadastral map, currently house #5, the Budnar Museum House.
  • Franz and Jacob Osredkar still living at SP3 through 1859 when they each get married.
  • Jacob Osredkar (b. 1810), age 32, married Ursula Todbecasheg/Podbnugsnig, age 38, SP6, 14 Feb 1843 (VP 03566 pg7). Correction 2026-07-31: an earlier draft of this note had the bride as “Ursula Urankar?, SP1” — the newly-cited marriage record gives her name as Todbecasheg/Podbnugsnig, from SP6. Jacob was only 16 at time of 1827 survey.
  • Franz Osredkar (b. ~1823), age 36, married Ursula Blaly/Mlaly, age 17, SP10, 17 Jun 1859 (VP 03566 pg13). Ursula was daughter of unmarried Maria Blaly. Update 2026-08-06 (Gary, unconfirmed): Ursula had an illegitimate daughter Maria, b. 25 May 1859 at SP10 (before the marriage). Franz reportedly died 6 July 1861 — a specific date, but no Matricula citation yet. Ursula’s mother identified as Maria Mlaly, unmarried, of Bela Peč 2 (south and west of the Bela Peč 3 Osredkar/Pistotnik farm, neighboring it) — but Gary flags a competing candidate, a same-named “Maria Malli” family at Zg Palovče 10 itself (Ursula’s own recorded birthplace), and isn’t sure which is the actual mother. This death date sharpens an existing contradiction, doesn’t resolve it: Franz is also recorded fathering a son (Franz Jr) at Gozd parish in 1863, two years after this reported death — see Acorn question f28384f0, updated 2026-08-06.
  • A Bernoth infant death at SP3, age 5 mos, also noted in records.
  • Godparent “Maria Dollens” appears for later children — may indicate a change in Helena’s circle or a different Maria serving as godmother.

DISCREPANCIES & RESEARCH NOTES

  1. House numbering changed: Early records may show SP4 instead of SP3 - numbering shifted around 1800
  2. Georg’s exact origin: Marriage record hard to read; confirmed via godparent records showing “vulgo Osredkar”
  3. Two Maria Osredkars: Georg’s daughter Maria AND Maria Osredkar who married Joseph Vaupetic (from Bela Pec) - NOT siblings, but grew up across valley from each other and both married into Palovce same year (1796)
  4. Marriage date discrepancy: Worksheet originally listed 3 Feb 1796, but this may be Simon Rems’s marriage date. SP families chart says Georg & Helena married 25 Apr 1796. Two different source links: Kamnik 00733 vs VP 03564 pg6. Needs re-examination of original records.
  5. Michael’s birth month: CSV reads “10 ber 1801” which likely means October (Latin abbreviation for 8th month in old calendar, but commonly October in these records). Earlier worksheet had “10 Aug 1801.”
  6. Budnar Museum connection: Francisca (daughter of Joseph Koshir and Gertrud Kladnik at SP5) married Budnar in 1874 per Museum website. She would have been 28. Francisca’s mother Gertrud Kladnik (b. 1820) had mother Ursula Rems from Trob 1 — no confirmed connection yet to SP Rems families. The house became the Budnar Museum via this line, not through the Osredkar ownership.
