  • “Vulgo” (known as) indicates the house/property name
  • Married TWICE:
    1. Unknown first wife (before 1769) - presumably died
    2. Barbara Kuhar (25 Jan 1769)
  • Death record same page as brother Gregor (age 57, Apr 1806)
  • 2026-09-04: Matola.ged GEDCOM cross-match — FSFTID GD7S-7XX attached, independently corroborating father Mathias Osredkar/Liuk (b.1715, FSFTID GFMD-RRL, also attached), wife Barbara Kuhar, and all 4 children below via family-link data.   • 2026-09-23: marriage record also gives Martin’s residence as “Lower St. Martin, Tuhinj” — new detail, not previously documented.

Wife Notes

  • Kuhar is a common surname in the region
  • Godmother 13 Oct 1769 (2026-11-08, full read from zoom, HIGH): Lucas, son of Sebastian Hribar × Helena Hozhevar, s Terbeunga — “Levant. Mathia Osredkar, et Barbara Martini Osredkar uxor.” (Šmartno baptisms 03361 scan 39, right page). The Mathias is her father-in-law (b. 1715) or Mathias Jr (b. 1747) — not stated. No child of hers yet by then; her first, Maria, was born 27 May 1770 (03361 p.41 sc42)
  • Godmother again 10 Jun 1789 (2026-11-09, full read from zoom, HIGH): Maria, dau. of Sebastianus Hribar × Helena Hozhevarza, Trobeuo — “Martin Osredkar et Barbara ejus uxor” (Šmartno baptisms 03362 p.31 scan 32). Same Hribar × Hozhevar couple as the 1769 baptism above, so the identification is strong. Latest known alive date (Acorn a37b2ecf, high). A “Barbara Osretkarza” (no husband named) is godmother 18 Jun 1789 on the same page (MEDIUM-LOW that it is her). Death search (2026-11-09, in progress): Sela deaths 04366 Jan 1793 – Dec 1798 (sc2–9, pp.1–8) NEGATIVE for Barbara (every entry read; no Barbara Osredkar/Liuk, no adult woman at Sovinja Peč 5). Šmartno 03368 Jul 1790 – Dec 1793 probably negative (the 11-08 pass logged every Sovinja Peč entry; no SP5 woman), but that pass was for Apollonia. Jun 1789 – Jul 1790 largely falls in Šmartno’s unrecorded gap. Ruled out (LOW): “Barbara Dollinshik”, Markou 6, 52, d. 9 Apr 1798 (Sela 04366 p.8 sc9; full read from zoom) — right age, but a Dolinšek of Markovo; the possible bridge (a “Martinus Dolinshik” at Sovina Pezh 5 in 1788) failed: his wife is Ursula (03362 sc30, zoom), not Barbara. Next: Sela 04366 from sc10 (1799) to ~1812; Martinus’s own death is at p.15 sc16 (1805).
  • SP5 occupants (2026-11-09, collateral): 8 Mar 1788 “Martinus Dolinshik et Ursula Osouniskin(?)”, son Gregorius, Sovina Pezh N° 5 (03362 p.28 sc30; mother’s given name HIGH); 26 Mar 1797 “Lukas Urh”, male, 105(?), Souinapetsch 5 (Sela 04366 p.6 sc7). So by 1788 SP5 had other residents beside (or instead of) Martinus × Barbara, who were at “Liukovu N. 5” in 1786. Check Martinus’s house in his 1805 entry.
  • Identified from children’s birth records as “Barbara, of Mathai Kuhar”
  • 2026-09-23, live-verified via 2 independent screenshots: Šmartno v Tuhinju 03371 pg.8 (groom’s home parish) and Nevlje 03729 pg.22 (bride’s home parish) — both independently corroborating the marriage and naming Barbara’s parents as Mathias Kuhar & Agnes Kuhar. Minor unconfirmed flag: a witness “Jacobo Terbolshagk” on this record shares a surname with Marina Terboshag, wife of Mathias Osredkar/Liuk (G06) — noted, not asserted as a family connection.

Children Notes

  • Georg (our ancestor) moved ~2 hours away to Zgornje Palovce when he married Helena Rems in 1796
  • Multiple children died young (common for the era)
  • Checked 2026-11-07 (Larry’s zoom): the 31 Mar 1780 burial is NOT this family’s. Šmartno deaths 03367 scan 10 reads “Paulus + Mathiae Osredkar militis Leg. fil.” — Paul, legitimate son of the late (+) Mathias Osredkar, soldier — Savina Petsh 5, age 2 years. So it is Paul b. 24 Jan 1778 (see G06), not infant Mathias. Mathias (b. 1780) actually died 9 Dec 1784, aged 4 (see row 3).

Test: Šmartno baptisms 03362, 1780–1784, Sovinja Peč 5. Not in Acorn.
