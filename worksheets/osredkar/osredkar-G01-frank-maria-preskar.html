Wife Notes

  • From Höberg/Podsreda parish in Kozjansko region
  • Parents: Anton Preskar (b. 1841) & Apolonia Preskar (b. 1842)
  • Grandparents: Franz Preskar & Agnes Dobrautz; Thomas Preskar & Apollonia Gersina
  • Earliest known ancestor: Thomas Preskar (b. 1735) & Magdalena Schigouski (from GEDCOM)
  • Had 12 siblings (many died in infancy)
  • Birth record: Matricula Online - Podsreda 01937 pg176
  • At Ludmila’s birth (1893, Zenica), Maria’s origin was recorded as “Understaermark, Štajerska” (Lower Styria) — i.e., the Slovenian/Austrian Styria region, not yet associated with the Osredkar surname (see Marriage Notes).
  • Obituary found 2026-09-09 — Larry located a full English translation (from his own old emails, sourced via dlib.si, URN:NBN:SI:DOC-AUNO4Z7T) of her “Naznanilo in Zahvala” (Announcement and Thanksgiving) notice — confirmed via dlib.si’s own item metadata to be from Ameriška Domovina (Cleveland, Vol. 29, Issue 147), dated 19 Dec 1927 (the notice itself is dated “Cleveland, Ohio, December 15, 1927” — written 4 days before publication). A different paper from “Amerikanski Slovenec,” which carried her brother John’s 1937 obituary — don’t conflate the two. Funeral home Grdina & Sons; officiating priests Ponikvar, Jagr, and Virant; parish confraternity Society of the Heart of Mary. States she died “October 9 at 9:15 a.m.” — conflicted by one day with the FindAGrave-sourced “10 October 1927,” but resolved 2026-09-09: Larry separately downloaded dlib.si’s raw OCR text of the issue (source-docs/slovenian-names/Amerika_URN_NBN_SI_DOC-AUNO4Z7T.txt) and the original Slovenian was read directly — “ki je preminula dne 9. oktobra ob 9:15 dopoldne” confirms the translation exactly. Given the precision (exact time) and contemporary, family-authored nature of this source, 9 October now supersedes the FindAGrave date (Acorn claim 83618f63 upgraded to confirmed; 354e4df6 downgraded to medium, kept on file not deleted). As of her death, survived in Slovenia by her father (age 86 — matches Anton Preskar’s confirmed 1841 birth almost exactly, and confirms he was alive as of Dec 1927, consistent with his own 1937 death), five sisters, and one brother; in America by her brother John, in Auburn, Illinois (see [[project-john-presker-illinois-newspaper-lead-2026-09-09]] — his own 1937 obituary was found the same day). The “five sisters and one brother” in Slovenia isn’t yet reconciled against the 9 non-infant siblings already tracked in Anton & Apolonia’s family — open question.
  • Death certificate found 2026-09-09 (source-docs/slovenian-names/Death Certificate M osredkar 1927.jpg) — Ohio Dept. of Health, Cuyahoga County, File No. 55849. Independently corroborates the 9 Oct 1927, 9:15am death time (physician’s own certification), and adds: burial 12 Oct 1927 at Calvary Cemetery, undertaker A. Grdina & Son (matches the obituary exactly), cause of death myocarditis with mitral insufficiency (5yr duration). The physician signed the certificate on Oct 10, 1927 — plausibly the actual origin of the FindAGrave “10 October” date (a conflation of signature date with death date). Two fields conflict with well-established records, flagged not resolved: (1) birth date given as “March 25, 1867” vs. the Matricula-confirmed 21 May 1867 — ordinary informant imprecision on a death certificate, not treated as overriding the parish record; (2) father named “John Preskar,” not Anton — Larry’s own reaction: “it also calls her father John not Anton.” Working hypothesis, unconfirmed: informant Frank Osredkar (grieving, likely never having met his Slovenian father-in-law) may have named her brother John Preskar (Auburn, IL — the one relative in America he knew personally) instead of her actual father. This also explains the “preskar recovered nov” spreadsheet’s “Presumptive Maria… death record says her father’s name is John” note (sheet1.csv row 121) — it was about this exact certificate. Anton Preskar’s parentage of Maria is NOT being changed on the strength of this alone.

Marriage Notes

  • Ludmila was illegitimate when born (18 Sep 1893, Zenica) — registered under mother Marija Preskar’s surname. The Zenica parish birth book contains “no note of any man legitimising her” (per Slovenian family sheet research, 10 Oct 2022).
  • This means Frank and Maria were not yet married at Ludmila’s birth. The marriage must have occurred between Sep 1893 and Sep 1895 (the second child Elisabeta was born 14 Sep 1895 with Frank registered as father).
  • At Ludmila’s birth, Maria’s origin recorded as “Understaermark, Štajerska” (Lower Styria) — not yet linked by record to Frank Osredkar.
  • Frank was working in Bosnia (railway-related occupations) when they married.
  • Marriage record likely in Sarajevo civil/church records 1893–1895; Zenica is also possible if Maria returned there briefly.
  • Search window narrowed: 1893–1895, Sarajevo (priority) or Zenica.
