Marriage Notes

  • Ludmila was illegitimate when born (18 Sep 1893, Zenica) — registered under mother Marija Preskar’s surname. The Zenica parish birth book contains “no note of any man legitimising her” (per Slovenian family sheet research, 10 Oct 2022).
  • This means Frank and Maria were not yet married at Ludmila’s birth. The marriage must have occurred between Sep 1893 and Sep 1895 (the second child Elisabeta was born 14 Sep 1895 with Frank registered as father).
  • At Ludmila’s birth, Maria’s origin recorded as “Understaermark, Štajerska” (Lower Styria) — not yet linked by record to Frank Osredkar.
  • Frank was working in Bosnia (railway-related occupations) when they married.
  • Marriage record likely in Sarajevo civil/church records 1893–1895; Zenica is also possible if Maria returned there briefly.
  • Search window narrowed: 1893–1895, Sarajevo (priority) or Zenica.
