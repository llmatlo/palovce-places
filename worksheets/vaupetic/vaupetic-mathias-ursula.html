Husband Notes

  • Parents identified (per houses(2) and Early Palovce chart notes): Mathias is son of Mathias Vaupetic (b. 1707) and Magdalena Bernioth (married 17 Jan 1735). Magdalena was a daughter of Blasy and Helena Bernioth of Rabensburg. Acorn IDs: father (see Mathias Luca Vaupetic b.1707), mother 653f0c06-78df-415e-8126-d39ff9922ec7, this Mathias 3ab11107-3fbf-4d33-8f1a-864b5db5c38e.
  • Sister: Gertrudis Vaupetic, b. Mar 1742 (Kamnik 00710 pg63) — same parents.
  • Source: “father Mathais more clearly at Inf P” (Inferior Palovce)
  • Vaupetic family prominent in Palovce area; SP1 known as “Vaupetic house”
  • Marriage to Ursula Rak: 15 Jan 1767, Kamnik #128 (parents listed: Mathias & Magdalena Vaup his side; Andreas & Agnetis Rak hers).
  • Disambiguation correction: Earlier note speculated “Mathia Vaup & Ursula Rak” might be a son of this Mathias — that is now resolved as the SAME person. He was previously thought to be the father of Georgius Vaupetitsch (b. ~1775, SP2), but houses(2) corrects this: Georgius is son of Michael Vaupetic & Ursula Rems, not of this Mathias × Ursula Rak couple.

VAUPETIC FAMILY CONTEXT

From source documents: - “There are so many Vaupetic families and they live in so many places” - “Interestingly, they only own three houses” - SP1 is known as the “Vaupetic house” - SP2 is called “Pirc House” in some records

The Vaupetic family is widespread but their exact relationships are complex and not fully documented.
