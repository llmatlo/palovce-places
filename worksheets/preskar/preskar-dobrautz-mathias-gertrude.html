CRITICAL NOTE — surname alias at Hbg 5

The Dobrautz family at Hbg 5 also appears in Podsreda parish records under the surnames Speleiz / Speliz and Marktler. Two children’s baptismal entries name the father as “Georgius Speliz, not Dobrautz” (Agnes 1779) and the mother’s death entry as “Speliz not Dobrautz” (Maria, March 1781). Records filed only under “Speliz” or “Marktler” must be searched alongside “Dobrautz.” Modern Slovenian forms: Dobravc (Dobrautz), Valenčak (Valentschak / Valanshag), and possibly Dolenšek (Dalenschak).

This alias was not previously documented in the GEDCOM and is a new investigation lead.

Independent corroboration, 2026-09-15: a parish grain-stock ledger (1810-1818, bound into the same Matricula volume as death register 01946 — see [[reference-matricula-page-number-unreliable]]) lists Hörberg House No. 5 under “Matthäus Schmiz, vulgo Schpelitsch” — confirming “Speliz” (spelled “Schpelitsch” here) as the house’s own vulgo name, persisting even after ownership passed to a different family by 1810. Grain quantities (Korn/Türken/Waitz/Gerste/Haber) for this house, 1810-1818, captured but not yet transcribed — see preskar-thomas-magdalena.md Historical Context for the House 6 companion find.
