Bracketed words are filled in to match the standard Austrian Catholic marriage-register consent/banns formula of this era, not directly read off the page. Only the un-bracketed words are read with MEDIUM confidence.

[Nach] Einwilligung [der Eltern, so wie] beg[onnen] [und] [gemäß] und einem obrigkeitlich[en Verlaß], [be]h[u]fs [der] Heirat[h] [im Jahre] 1830, [den] Nov[ember] 1830, [wurden die Eltern] vernommen, nicht [erreichter] Volljährigkeit [wegen], [des] Bräutigam[s] [und der Braut] kommt in Betracht, wegen ob[ig]en Grund[es] [ist die Einwilligung] gegeben [worden].

[Es] wird [von] diesem Verhältnis[se] [drei]mal gänzlich [bekannt] gemacht, bis [a]n [den] Sonntag als d[en] 13., 20ten, [und] 27ten November, v[el]. [gerechnet] a[ls] 2[0]., 24., und 2[7]. Sonntag nach Pfingsten, [wurde die] Verkündigung von der Kanzel[,] und gegen ihre Einwilligung von den Eltern, und [i]n Ansehung der Verbindung kein Hindernis nicht vorhanden, wurden [sie ge]traut [von] [Pfarrer] [signature illegible].

Plain-English sense (paraphrase, not a literal translation): With the parents’ consent, and by official authorization — because the bride and/or groom had not yet reached legal age of majority — the banns for this marriage were publicly announced three times, on 13, 20, and 27 November (the 20th, 24th, and 27th Sunday after Pentecost). The parents’ consent was given, and no impediment to the union was found. They were married by the parish priest (signature illegible).

Confidence: the anchor words (Einwilligung, Obrigkeitlich, Volljährigkeit, Bräutigam, Sonntag nach Pfingsten, the date numbers, Einwilligung von den Eltern, kein Hindernis vorhanden) are MEDIUM confidence. Every bracketed word is a template reconstruction to make the sentence cohere, not a verified read — a future re-check with a tighter crop of just this clause could confirm or overturn any of it.
