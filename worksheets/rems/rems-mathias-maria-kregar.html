✓ TWO-MATHIAS HYPOTHESIS RESOLVED (2026-05-16)

This worksheet documents Mathias Rems b. Feb 1739, son of Martinus & Barbara Rems, who married Ursula Berlz in 1761 and then Maria Kregar in 1776 after Ursula’s death. He is Helena Rems’s father (Helena b. 9 May 1777 → m. Georg Osredkar 1796 → mother of the Osredkar direct line).

⚠️ Database drift found and fixed 2026-08-01: despite this worksheet being correct since May, Acorn’s actual structured family record for the Maria Kregar marriage — the one that determines who Helena’s father is in the database, not just in prose — was still pointing at the rejected Mathias (b.1733, Antony’s son) the entire time. Two related claims (this Mathias’s birth date, and Ursula Berlz’s death date) also still held their old pre-resolution values despite their own notes describing the May refinement. All three are now corrected: new family 35c9c618 (Mathias × Maria Kregar) with all 5 children moved over, old wrong family c42a792c deleted, both claims replaced with correct values, and person 81326ca2’s (the rejected Mathias) notes corrected to stop asserting the debunked marriage. See hypothesis ccb3af62’s notes for the full account.

An older draft of this worksheet split him into two candidate identities and treated Antony & Catharina’s son (b. 9 Feb 1733) as the more likely father. That identification has been rejected. Antony’s son married Lucia Wesjak in 1764, and Lucia did not die until 11 Jan 1799 at Rabensburg 6 (VP 03561 pg9) — therefore Antony’s Mathias was never a widower in 1776 and could not have married Maria Kregar. See Two-Mathias Hypothesis for the full evidence chain.

The Antony’s-son Mathias is documented as a collateral figure in rems-antony-catharina.md. His 1810 SP4 death is best explained by him moving to SP4 after his wife Lucia’s 1799 death.
