Family ID: REMS-GREGOR-HELENA Acorn family: ccd3c9d4 Location: Ravne 10 (Dumf Rauno 10) Parish: Kamnik Time Period: 1833-1897 (children’s births/deaths now span 1833-1855) Worksheet Created: April 2026 Data Confidence: MEDIUM - Marriage record confirmed; children from birth records at Kamnik

Primary source records: Matricula Online (data.matricula-online.eu), Nadškofijski arhiv Ljubljana.

OVERVIEW

Gregor Rems married Helena Kregar (Gerkman) on 18 February 1833. They lived at Ravne 10, which may have been Helena’s property. Their son Bartholomeus married Apollonia Grill in the famous 1865 double wedding, connecting this collateral Rems branch to the Osredkar direct line.

This is a COLLATERAL branch — not part of the direct SP3/SP4 Palovce Rems line. Barthol Rems is definitively not descended from the SP4 Rems, based on three independent lines of evidence: different grandmother, different geography, different social network, and chronological impossibility.

Wife Notes

  • RESOLVED 2026-10-17: Gerkman is her birth surname. The 1833 entry gives her as Helena Kregar, widow, daughter of Oswald Gerkman, Hübler. Kregar was her first married name. Per Gary’s 2022 narrative (not yet verified by us): first husband Johann Kregar of Raune 10, m. 16 Nov 1829, d. 7 Sep 1832. Their son Oswald Kregar (b. 10 Aug 1831) was evidently named after her father.
  • Ravne 10 was her first husband’s Kregar house, which Gregor married into
  • Correction (2026-10-17): she was not a Kregar by blood, so 1833 is not a true third Kregar–Rems intermarriage. It is only a marriage into a Kregar household. The two earlier Kregar–Rems marriages in Research Insights are:
    • 1693: Helena Kregar married Lucas Rems (SP2) — 1st Kregar-Rems intermarriage
    • 1776: Maria Kregar married Mathias Rems (SP4) — 2nd Kregar-Rems intermarriage
    • 1833: Helena Kregar Gerkman married Gregor Rems (Ravne 10) — 3rd Kregar-Rems intermarriage (born Gerkman; Kregar only by her first marriage)
  • This 140-year pattern of Kregar-Rems marriages reflects the endogamy of small rural Slovenian communities
  • Helena Kregar Gerkman is a different woman from Helena Kregar of SP4 (who married Lucas Rems ~1693, over a century earlier)

SIGNIFICANCE

1865 Double Wedding Connection

Gregor and Helena’s son Bartholomeus married Apollonia Grill on 13 February 1865, the same day that Apollonia’s sister Maria Grill married Mathias Osredkar. This double wedding united: - The collateral Rems branch (Gregor’s line from Sidrash Lokalin/Ravne 10) - The Osredkar direct line (through Maria Osredkar’s son Mathias) - The Grill/Pistotnik family (both sisters were daughters of Stephan Grill & Anna Pistotnik)

Collateral Branch Confirmation

Three independent discriminators confirm this is NOT the SP4 Rems line: 1. Different grandmother: Helena Kregar Gerkman vs. the SP4 Helena Kregar (married Lucas Rems ~1693) 2. Different geography: Ravne 10 and Sidrash Lokalin, not Palovce 3. Different social network: No Bernoth godparents (the unique discriminator for Antony’s SP4 branch); Kamnik parish, not SP godparent circle

Kregar-Rems Endogamy Pattern

This marriage is the third documented Kregar-Rems intermarriage. Corrected 2026-10-17: Helena was born a Gerkman and was a Kregar only as Johann Kregar’s widow. So this is a Rems marrying into a Kregar household (Raune 10), not a third Kregar–Rems blood link. The two earlier ones (1693, 1776) stand.
