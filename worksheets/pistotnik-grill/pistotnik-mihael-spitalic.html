OVERVIEW

Mihael Pistotnik (b. ~1735) was the 3rd child of Josephus Pistotnik and Elizabeta Osredkar. He moved from Bela Peč 3 to Špitalič pri Motniku after marrying Uršula Pikel. The house name was “Clemen.” This branch extends 6 generations from Mihael to 20th century descendants, including two WWII concentration camp victims.

Parentage disputed (2026-10-19): Mihael’s link to Josephus Pistotnik × Elizabeta Osredkar comes only from Borut Pistotnik’s research (via pistotniki-osredkarji). No baptism has been found (the “~23 Feb 1735” is an estimate), and his 1753 marriage does not name his father. Gary DelNero has found a baptism for a different Michael Pistotnik further east that he thinks fits better. A birth in 1735 would also make him only ~18 at marriage. Open: check Gary’s record. Acorn person 38a3e8e2 (birth date and birthplace lowered to low).

1735 baptism sweep: NEGATIVE (2026-10-19). Every 1735 baptism in Šmartno v Tuhinju 03359, scans 88–94 was read page by page. That’s 57 entries (30 boys, 27 girls), matching the scribe’s own year-end total, so none was missed. Nothing near 23 Feb (20 Feb Mathias Vidmer, then 8 Mar).   • RESULT (2026-10-19): Gary’s candidate is ruled out.
    • Baptism verified: Michael, son of Andreas Pestotnig × Gertrudis, “ex Lok”, bapt. 7 May 1728, born the same day. Godparents Thomas Terbolnig and Marina ?Caneilzka. 03359 scan 49, p.48.
    • Death found: “Michaël filius legit. Andreae Pestotnig”, buried 25 Aug 1731, “aetatis annorum trium et 4 fere mensium” (3 years, almost 4 months). 03366 scan 49, left-hand unnumbered page facing p.48.
    • The age fits his birth to the month, so this child cannot be the Mihael who married in 1753.
  • No replacement Michael, 1732–1734: NEGATIVE (2026-10-19). Quick-pass sweep of 03359 scans 70–88. Checked against the scribe’s year totals: 1732 = 45, 1733 = 46, 1734 = 65 (my count 66, so nothing skipped). The only Michaels were sons of Matthaeus Savinshegg (27 Sep 1733, Pod Savine pezhjo) and Nicolaus Slemenshegg (28 Sep 1734). With the 1735 sweep, no Pistotnik Michael was baptised at Šmartno in 1731–1735. Mihael’s birth is either outside those years or outside the parish.

Leads from the sweep (single-pass reads, not citations): - Other Pistotnik households in the parish in 1735, any of which could have fathered “Michael Pistotnik, father unnamed”: - “Mathias Pestotnig seu Matte” × Magdalena, Loke: daughter Margaretha 10 Jul (scan 91). - “Mathias Gabrovish vulgo Matte” × Dorothea, Sredna vas: daughter Elisabeth 9 Oct (scan 92). Gabrovish = “from Gabria”, like Josephus’s father Mathias Pistotnik ex Gabria. - Godfathers “Matthaeus Matte seu/vulgo Pestotnig” (9 Feb scan 89, 25 Nov scan 93) and “Mathia Pestotnig seu Shobej” (30 Aug, scan 91). - Next test: their children c. 1730–40 for a Michael. - Clemenz is a real surname in the parish: Lucas Clemenz × Agnes, Sredna vas (4 Jun, scan 90); Georg Clemenz vulgo Shushter × Helena, Selih (18 Sep, scan 92). So the Špitalič “Clemen” house name may not come from Mihael’s own family. - For other lines (to be copied to those worksheets): - Georg Polanshek × Elisabetha, “pod Belo peshio”: daughter Gertrudis 10 Mar (scan 89); he is also godfather 29 Apr (scan 90). Relevant to BP3 Pistotnik→Poljanšek. - Primus Shumer vulgo Sagorishnig × Helena, Na Likovim: daughter Elisabeth 26 Oct (scan 92), with godparents Paul Terbolshag and Ursula Osredkarza. - Primus Berliz × Helena, Na Likovim: daughter Barbara 4 Dec (scan 93). - Mathias Podbelshig × Helena, below Bela Peč: son Bartholomaeus 17 Aug (scan 91), with Terbolshag godparents.

Note: This is a COLLATERAL branch — not in the direct Osredkar ancestry line. Our line descends from Bartholomew Pistotnik (b. 1767), Mihael’s half-brother from Josephus’s second marriage.
