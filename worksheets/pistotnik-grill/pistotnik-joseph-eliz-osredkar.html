SIGNIFICANCE

This 1730 marriage is KEY to understanding the Pistotnik/Osredkar connection:

  1. Joseph Pistotnik came from Gabria (Pistotnik family of Mathias)
  2. Elizabeta Osredkar was daughter of Luka Osredkar at Bela Pec 3
  3. Joseph married into the Osredkar farm and took on the house name
  4. Descendants used “Pistotnik fue Osredkar” or “Pistotnik/Osredkar”
  5. BUT: They are NOT blood descendants of Luka Osredkar - the connection is through marriage only
  6. The Bela Peč Osredkar line is DIFFERENT from the SP5/Liukovim Osredkar line (Gregorius → Joannes → etc.)
