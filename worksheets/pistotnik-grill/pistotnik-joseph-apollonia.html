Marriage Notes

  • Married under Kamnik parish, not Šmartno
  • Joseph was widowed ~2 months prior (Elizabeta died 3.12.1749) — the marriage record itself explicitly names him “Viduus” (widower), independently confirming this
  • New detail, 2026-09-24 live re-verification: the record gives Joseph’s specific prior residence as “Vellak,” near the St. Agnes chapel, in the vicariate of St. Martin in Tuhinj. Witnesses named: Mathia Kubej, Antonio Pistolnig (a second Pistotnik-surname witness, relationship unconfirmed), and Simon Thaddäus Reuh (chaplain). Groom’s surname spelled “Pistolnig” in this record — a plausible phonetic/scribal variant, not a separate identity.

LINEAGE CLARIFICATION

Previous worksheets incorrectly stated “Joseph/Blais (b. 1751)” as the father of Bartholomew.

The new source document clarifies: - Blasius (b. 1751) and Bartholomew (b. 1767) are BROTHERS, both sons of Joseph and Apolonija - Bartholomew is the younger brother, NOT the son of Blasius

PISTOTNIK/OSREDKAR NAME USAGE

The source document notes that: - Children were registered with varying surnames: “Pistotnik, Osredkar” or just “Osredkar” - Joseph was known as “Pistotnik, po domače Osredkar” (Pistotnik, by house name Osredkar) - The family adopted the Osredkar house name but retained Pistotnik surname in records
