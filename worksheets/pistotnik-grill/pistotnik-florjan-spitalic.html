OVERVIEW

Florjan Pistotnik was a descendant of the Mihael Pistotnik Spitalic branch. His son Franc became a victim of the Jasenovac concentration camp in 1945. This worksheet documents the Florjan sub-branch separately from the main Mihael Spitalic worksheet due to the significance of the WWII history and geographic separation (Florjan’s descendants moved to Mozirje/Bosnia).

Note on Florjan’s placement: The Pistotniki-Osredkarji document lists Florjan as the father of Franc (b. 1892, Jasenovac victim) but does not provide Florjan’s own birth/death dates or specify which generation he belongs to. Based on his son Franc’s 1892 birth, Florjan was likely born ~1860s. He may be a son of Luka Pistotnik (Gen 4) rather than of Franc Pistotnik Sr. (Gen 5B). The exact placement needs further research.

Notes on Franc’s Deportation

  • Franc’s trajectory: Spitalic -> Mozirje -> Sarajevo -> Ilidza -> Jasenovac
  • This geographic path eerily mirrors Frank Osredkar’s voluntary migration 50 years earlier (Kamnik area -> Bosnia), but under horrific circumstances
  • Franc was arrested by Germans, then sentenced by the Ustasa (Croatian fascist) court — reflecting the complex political dynamics of occupied Yugoslavia
  • He died just as the war was ending; Jasenovac was liberated/destroyed in late April 1945
  • His wife Pavla Antonija Bergant was a teacher who survived the war (d. 1963)

WWII CONTEXT: PISTOTNIK FAMILY ACROSS THE WAR

The Spitalic Pistotnik branch was devastated by WWII. All victims descend from Josephus Pistotnik & Elizabeta Osredkar (the 1730 Bela Pec 3 founders):

Person Branch Fate Date
Franc Pistotnik Florjan sub-branch Died at Jasenovac 9 May 1945
Anton Klemen Johann sub-branch (married Marija Pistotnik) Died at Dachau 30 Mar 1945
Anton Klemen Jr Johann sub-branch Killed at Vransko (NOB) 28 Dec 1944
  • Father and son Klemen died within 3 months of each other
  • Franc Pistotnik and Anton Klemen Sr. died within 10 days of each other at two different concentration camps
  • The family was caught between German occupation forces, Ustasa collaborators, and Partisan resistance
