Purpose of This Document

Gary’s notes accompany his master chart of all families at Zgornje Palovce (Superior Palovce), a settlement of about half a dozen homes near Kamnik. Starting in 2021, he attempted to untangle the family trees of every household, originally covering ~1780 to the mid-1900s. A December 2022 Facebook post from “Jan,” a newly discovered cousin with ancestors at SP4, prompted him to revise the chart, pushing further back in time. This document records his dead-ends, assumptions, and house-by-house analysis.

Key Methodology: House Number Reassignment

A critical finding underlies the entire chart revision:

House numbers shifted by one digit around 1800. Before ~1800, families appear to have had house numbers one digit higher than their 1827 cadastral survey numbers. Gary applied this correction throughout:

Open discrepancy (2026-08-14): Gary’s more recent note dates this shift to ~1812 rather than ~1800. Not yet reconciled — could be a redating of the same event, or a second/later renumbering episode distinct from this one. See sp8-sweep-checklist.md Tier 3.

This means earlier worksheets and the Osredkar homestead narrative that reference “SP4” for Helena Rems’s birth actually place her at what became SP3 (the Budnar House Museum) in 1827.

Challenging Ancestors

The Two Martin Rems (b. ~1715)

The most critical unresolved question for the SP3/SP4 connection:

Candidate Birth Parents Source
Martinus 2 Nov 1714 Sebastian Rems & Gertrudis Kamnik 00708 pg63
Marting 1 Nov 1715 Luca Rems & Helena Kamnik 00708 pg73

Evidence: - Many children born to Martini & Barbara; only one to Martini & Helena (apparently illegitimate, 1735) - No marriage record found for either Martin & Barbara - Death records 1714-1731 searched — second Martin not found - “Our” Martin died 1771 at SP4, age ~60 - Easy answer: Luca & Helena — these are also parents of Anthony Rems (SP4), so the farm would split between brothers - Difficult answer: Sebastian & Gertrudis — means SP3 and SP4 families don’t share an ancestor until Gregor (before 1620s)

Overlapping Families

Gary found instances where two births were recorded to the same father and mother within a few months — indicating two different couples with the same names:

Thomas Rems & Agnetis: Two births Aug and Nov 1718 with different godparents Mathias Rems: Both father and godfather in overlapping 1692 records (Agnes b. Jan 1692 to Mathia & Gertrud; Helena b. Mar 1692 to a different couple with Mathia as godfather)

Urankar/Rems Alias

Thomas Urankar appears linked to the Rems family: - Thomas Urankar m. Dorothea Remska, 1695 - Thomas Urankar (widow) m. Agnetis Blais Bernoth, 18 Nov 1715 - “Rems fue Urankar” or “known as” appears in records - Joannes (Thoma & Agnete Rems) fue Urankar — confirming the alias

Kregar Family Connections

Both SP3 and SP4 connect to the Kregar family of Kregarjevo (north of Kamnik):

Sebastian Kregar (Kregarjevo)
    |
    +-- Helena Kregar (b. 1675)
    |       m. Lucas Rems, July 1693 (SP4)
    |       --> Antony Rems --> Primus --> Gregor --> Thomas/Joseph (SP4)
    |
    +-- Antonius Kregar
            |
            +-- Urbanus Kregar
                    m. Miza Urbani Preklet
                    |
                    +-- Maria Kregar
                            m. Mathias Rems, 1776 (SP3)
                            --> Helena Rems (b. 1777) --> m. Georg Osredkar

Thus: From Helena Rems (b. 1777), descendants of both SP3 and SP4 are related through the Kregar line, with Sebastian Kregar as the common ancestor — 2G grandfather to both SP3’s Helena and SP4’s Gregor.

Rudnik Kregar Connection

  • Rudnik is southwest of Palovce, in Wolfsbach cadastral area
  • Laurentius Luca Vaup (SP1) m. Agatha Joanis Kregar of Rudnik, 1744
  • Blais (Laurenti) Kregar, Rudnik, m. Maria (Primi) Rems of SP4/5, 15 Sept 1770 — Maria is sister of Jan’s Gregor Rems
  • Blais died at Rudnik Mar 1775, age 29

Rabensburg/Vranja Pec Notes

  • VP parish index from 1789 shows all Rems births at SP until Johann, Mar 1844, at Rabensburg 1 (Johann is youngest son of Simon Rems, SP8)
  • VP 1 identified in 1827 as the “sexton’s house” (meznarija)
  • In the 1730s-50s, Blais Berniot “fue Rems” married and had family in Vranja Pec
  • Blais (of Luca Rems & Helen) m. Miza Michaelis Bernioth, 1737 — unclear why “fue Rems” and not “fue Bernioth”
  • A Mathias Rems died at Rabensburg
